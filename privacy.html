Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 30, 2026

1. Data we access

With the operator's own OAuth consent, ActiveVibeStats accesses only the operator's own TikTok account data via TikTok's official Display API: basic profile info, profile statistics (followers, likes, video count), and the operator's own video list with counters (views, likes, comments, shares, publish time).

2. Data we do not collect

We do not collect, store, or share personal data of any other individual. We do not use advertising trackers, analytics cookies, or third-party SDKs on this site.

3. Storage

API tokens are stored only on the operator's own machine. This website itself stores nothing about visitors; it serves static legal documents only.

4. Data sharing

No data is sold or shared with third parties.

5. Contact

Questions about this policy: via the TikTok account @ActiveVibeSports.
